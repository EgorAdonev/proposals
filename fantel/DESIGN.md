# Fantel Design Context

## Brand Voice

Warm, production-grade, buyer-focused. Food product imagery should feel real and appetizing, but interface copy must speak to procurement risk, logistics, assortment, and conversion.

## Visual Direction

Keep Fantel green and gold as the identity anchor. Use a light cream base, deep green typography, restrained gold accents, and B2B-table clarity. Avoid generic blue SaaS styling, glass cards, gradient text, and decorative orbs.

## Typography

Use a strong serif for headlines to echo Fantel packaging warmth, paired with a readable sans for dense tables and commercial copy. Body text should stay compact and scannable.

## Components

Use proof strips, comparison tables, KPI blocks, audit cards, semantic tables, and screenshot frames. Cards are allowed for repeated audit findings and documents, not for every section wrapper.

## Motion

Small reveal transitions only. No layout-moving animation. Proposal must remain readable as a GitHub Pages document.
