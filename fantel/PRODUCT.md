# Fantel Proposal Context

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Proposal site for ООО «КФ «ФАНТЭЛЬ» and Neat Studio. The page must explain why the current kf-fantel.com should be rebuilt from an image catalogue into a B2B sales instrument for retail chains, distributors, HoReCa, cafes, hotels, petrol stations, and regional partners.

## Audience

B2B decision makers: owners and category managers of regional retail, distributors, HoReCa buyers, cafe and hotel operators, and Fantel stakeholders approving the redesign budget.

## Core Message

Fantel is a Chelyabinsk producer of biscuit-cream cakes, pastries, and desserts. Since 2008 it has production, laboratory control, cold-chain logistics at +4/-18 C, and Russian regional supply potential. The new site should convert this into catalog requests, price requests, partner applications, and segment-specific landing pages.

## Strategic Principles

- Lead with commercial action: wholesale catalog, price request, assortment selection, partner application.
- Separate buyer segments from suppliers and HR.
- Turn catalogue data into procurement data: SKU, weight, shelf life, temperature, packaging, box quantity, documents.
- Make proof visible: quality documents, lab control, awards, client logos where allowed, geography, logistics.
- Build SEO and Yandex Direct structure around B2B clusters: wholesale cakes, wholesale pastries, HoReCa desserts, dessert supplier, regional demand.

## Anti References

Generic corporate brochure, long atmospheric hero copy, catalogue without filters, partner page that talks only to suppliers, decorative visuals that hide purchasing information.
